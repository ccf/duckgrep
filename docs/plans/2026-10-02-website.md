# duckgrep.dev: implementation plan

Spec: `docs/specs/2026-10-02-website.md`. Branch: `site/duckgrep-dev`. One PR.

## Steps

1. **Replay data.** Write `site/tools/extract_replay.py`.
   - Input: a task id and a setup, read from `bench/eval/runs/full/<task>/<setup>-1.jsonl.gz` and `results.jsonl`.
   - Output: each run's terminal, written into `site/index.html` between its markers: the steps (a tool name, its input, its output trimmed to 8 lines with a count of the rest, and the cumulative tokens and cost after the step as `data-*`), its answer and its totals.
   - Run it for the flask and ripgrep pairs and commit the page.
2. **Page skeleton.** `site/index.html` in its final, static state: every section from the spec, the terminals rendered by the extractor, meta and OG tags, and the favicons.
3. **Styles.** `site/site.css` on the brand tokens and `components.css` (terminal, button): the layout, the race grid stacking at 760px, the table and the `details` styling. Dark mode comes from the tokens.
4. **Replay.** `site/site.js`, about 200 lines with the tabs and copy buttons.
   - On view, it hides the steps and resets the counters, then reveals the steps in order at the runs' real pacing (each run took 6–8 seconds).
   - The counters tween to each step's cumulative values and freeze. A "Replay" button restarts it, and a tab switches to the Rust pair.
   - It respects `prefers-reduced-motion`, and does nothing without JavaScript.
5. **Build.** `site/build.sh` copies into `_site/`; add `_site/` to `.gitignore`.
6. **Tests.** `tests/test_site.py`, as the spec describes.
7. **Verify once.** Serve `_site` locally and take screenshots at 1440 and 390, light and dark, then reduced motion and with JavaScript off. Fix everything in one batch, then confirm once.
8. **Finish.** Run the design detector on `site/`, then the finish review, then write `DESIGN.md` for the site. Note the Cloudflare Pages settings in the PR body.
9. **PR.** Commit through the hook, push, open the PR, wait for the bots, address their comments, and merge when clean.

## Who does what

- **Steps 1–6 (the build):** one builder agent (Opus, high effort), because the page is design-led and judgement-heavy. Or I build it in this session, which keeps the design context. Recommended: in this session.
- **The review:** one independent reviewer on the finished PR diff (Opus, xhigh), to check the claims against `bench/RESULTS.md` and the brand rules.
